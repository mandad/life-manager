# Performance reporting guide

The [[../Documents/Work log|Work log]] is the raw feed for performance-review write-ups. Every entry should be written so that, at review time, it drops into the right evaluation category with zero rewriting — action verb, quantified scope, stated impact, category tag. If your review framework publishes **written descriptors for the top rating**, entries earn their keep by *evidencing* that descriptor's language.

## 1. Plug in your framework

Paste (or have Claude extract from your evaluation form) each category your review grades:

| Code | Category | What it measures | Top-rating descriptor language to echo |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{e.g. 3a}} | {{Planning}} | {{one-line ability definition}} | {{key phrases from the highest-rating description}} |

Also record any **length budgets** your form imposes per narrative section — the log can be long, but each entry should already be compressed and load-bearing.

*(No formal framework? Use generic categories — Delivery · Initiative · Leadership · Communication · Judgment — and skip the descriptor-echo layer.)*

## 2. The bullet formula

> **[Strong action verb] + [concrete deliverable] + [quantified scope] + [outcome / impact] + [category tag]**

- **Verb first, past tense, no throat-clearing** — "drove," "authored," "closed," "stood up"; never "was involved in," "helped with."
- **Quantify or name the scope** — numbers beat adjectives. If a captured item has no number, get one at capture time (count, days, "first," "all," percentage).
- **State the impact clause** where one exists — it's what separates an accomplishment from a diary line.
- **Tag it**: primary category, then cross-tags — `[3c · also 5d]`. At review time, filter by tag → per-category lists.

## 3. Metrics to capture routinely

Count at the moment of capture: **counts** (items delivered, people trained, sessions run) · **timeliness** (deadline vs. delivery, "ahead of schedule," zero late) · **firsts/superlatives** ("first," "only," "adopted org-wide") · **efficiency** (backlog cleared, hours saved, manual step automated) · **people outcomes** (qualifications produced, growth enabled — name the mechanism) · **reach** (who saw it, where it now lives).

## 4. Language bank

For each framework category, keep a short list of its top-descriptor verbs and frames, and echo them **only when the fact supports them** — echo, don't copy wholesale; never inflate. A stretched claim contaminates the honest ones. *(Claude builds this section from your framework in §1.)*

## 5. Compilation workflow

1. **Daily:** each "Noted today" jot is reworded per §2 into the Completed buffer, tagged.
2. **Weekly:** the buffer compiles into the Work log week-section, tags kept; related items grouped into single-story bullets; the period-defining item gets a ⭐.
3. **Review season:** filter the log by tag → per-category accomplishment list → chain the strongest into narratives inside the length budgets, echoing the descriptor phrases the evidence supports. If a category is thin, mine the daily records for uncaptured acts *before* writing around the gap.

## 6. Cautions

- **Honest first** — every line must survive your reviewer reading it against the record.
- **Names of other people stay out of the vault** — role-level wording only.
- **Don't over-tag** — one primary + at most 2-3 cross-tags.
- **The Work log stays supervisor-facing** — no personal admin.
